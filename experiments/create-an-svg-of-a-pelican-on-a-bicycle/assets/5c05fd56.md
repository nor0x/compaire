**How it works**

* **Background gradient** – The `<linearGradient id="bgGrad">` fills the entire SVG, giving a calm sky‑blue‑to‑light‑green backdrop.  
* **Wheel shadow** – The `<filter id="wheelShadow">` creates a soft, blurred shadow beneath each wheel; the filter is applied to the two `<circle>` elements that represent the wheels.  
* **Caption** – The `<text>` element is centered horizontally (`x="50%"`) and placed near the bottom (`y="88%"`) so it sits just below the ground line.
